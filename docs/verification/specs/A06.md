## Step 1: backend-aware requests and bounded validation

`TestLiveRequestBackendValidation` first failed because LiveRequest had no Backend field. It now covers unknown/default/raw/Npcap/Pktmon selection, Pktmon's empty-address rule, local IPv4/IPv6 loopback for Npcap and duration/packet bounds at and above both limits. Raw capture entry points reject requests for other backends before touching a socket. Native.Status preserves explicit raw-capture permission/platform limitations. LiveResult adds loss-limit codes; contract mappings and generated models preserve the new fields.
