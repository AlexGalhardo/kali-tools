## What was verified

`internal/dnsinspect.Validate` rejects an IP name, type `ANY`, a fifth resolver, a duplicate resolver, an `http` DoH URL, DoH userinfo, empty checks, `encrypted` without an endpoint, timeouts 199 and 5001, and a fifth trust anchor. It accepts timeouts 200 and 5000, four resolvers, two DoH endpoints and four DS anchors. Error text does not echo the submitted name, resolver, endpoint or anchor.

`classifyTransport` returns `port53_unreachable` for port 53 when UDP timed out and TCP was refused or timed out, `unreachable` for the same pair on another port, and `timeout` when UDP timed out and TCP connected. It does not dial.

`github.com/miekg/dns v1.1.73` is required by `go.mod`. Its module-cache `LICENSE` is BSD-3-Clause. The only production import is `internal/dnsinspect/anchors.go`. `node scripts/notices.mjs` collected 144 Go modules, including `github.com/miekg/dns v1.1.73`, plus 14 frontend packages and 4 vendored skills.
